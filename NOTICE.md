Gain changes while a song plays glide over half a second instead of flushing the audio; 16-bit and float PCM. |

The progress line stays flat (no wave). Runs even with system animations off; Settings → Personalize → Whale sightings. Easter egg: tap "Now playing" seven times for a Whale sighting button (`res/menu/toolbar_playback.xml`, `res/drawable/ic_whale_sighting_24.xml`, new) beside Streaming mode, which summons a sighting at once or queues one behind the sighting already showing.
