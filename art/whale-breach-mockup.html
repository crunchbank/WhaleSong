Breach

Modelled on the motion of the reference animation, drawn with our own humpback. A breach is momentum: the whale races in just below the surface, tail beating hard, its bow wave humping the water, and keeps travelling forward the whole time. The water wells up where it is coming, then it bursts out steeply, nose first, tail kicking, throwing a skirt of spray. At the top it flings both long flippers out wide. It hangs for a moment, rolls over onto its back in the air as it flies on, flippers sweeping round with the roll and its tail rolling too, flashing the whole spread fluke for a moment, and comes crashing down on its back well ahead of where it came out, throat grooves up and tail curling, in a huge splash: a crown of spray, a tower of mist, a slick of white foam, and the biggest ripples of any sighting. Settings carried over: whale 1.5×, bulge 0.5×, ripple speed 0.75×. The panel is much taller than the others, because a breaching humpback towers over the bar.
