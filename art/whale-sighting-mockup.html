Whale sightings: a proper humpback

Left of each pair is today's whale, right is the proposal. One side-on humpback is drawn once and every pose is that same animal posed and dipped under the water, so the bit you see is always real anatomy. It gets a thin outline in the background colour so it no longer melts into the swell, and the swell sits behind it at lower opacity. The progress bar is drawn as it really is: flat, with the whale in the played part, left of the thumb. The flipper is lighter, as a humpback's is.
