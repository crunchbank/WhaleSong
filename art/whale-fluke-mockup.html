Fluke up

The deep dive. After a last breath the whale arches its back high (the hump that gives humpbacks their name), rolls forward, and its tail stock climbs out of the water. As it rises the fluke turns to face us, edge-on to full spread, showing its pale underside: the same fluke as the app icon. It hangs a moment with water streaming and dripping off its serrated trailing edge, each drop rippling the sea, then slides straight down and is gone, leaving a smooth, calm patch, the fluke print. Settings carried over: whale 1.5×, bulge 0.5×, ripple speed 0.75×, out of the water 1.65×. The panel is taller than the others because the fluke stands well above the bar.
