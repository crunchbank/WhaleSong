Cruise with calf

A mother and her calf come up together and swim along the played part of the bar. The calf keeps station at her head, on the near side, paler than her as calves are, the way humpback calves ride beside their mothers. It is livelier than she is: it bobs faster, breathes more often, and dips under and pops back up while she keeps a steady course. Both leave a gentle wake. Then they sink away together, calf a beat behind. Settings carried over from surface and blow: whale 1.5×, bulge 0.5×, ripple speed 0.75×, out of the water 1.65×, tails kept under, stately sink.

The beats

  1. 0.5 s: the mother comes up, the calf half a second behind her.
  2. 2.2 s: the calf blows first, a small quick puff. 2.9 s: the mother blows.
  3. 3–10 s: they cruise. Each leaves a gentle wake from its head; the calf bobs faster than its mother.
  4. 5.4 s: the calf dips under, nose down, and comes back up with a splash of ripples and, at 8.3 s, another little blow.
  5. 10.4 s: they sink away slowly, the calf following a beat later, and the sea settles.
