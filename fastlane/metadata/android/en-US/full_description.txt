WhaleSong is a music player for the music on your phone. It is a modified version of <a href="https://github.com/OxygenCobalt/Auxio">Auxio</a>, and keeps everything that makes Auxio good: a fast, simple Material Design interface, playback built on Media3 ExoPlayer, gapless playback and full ReplayGain support. It works offline, has no ads and collects nothing.

<b>What WhaleSong adds</b>

<ul>
<li><b>Favorite albums.</b> Tap the heart on an album's page to give its cover a little coral heart, then tap the heart on the Albums tab to see only your favorites.</li>
<li><b>Cover grids.</b> Albums, artists, genres and playlists can show as a grid of covers, or as a list.</li>
<li><b>Streaming mode.</b> Plays every song at the same loudness, like streaming services, measuring songs that have no ReplayGain tags.</li>
<li><b>Play counts.</b> Sort by how often you've played something, and find what you've never played.</li>
<li><b>Playlist backups.</b> Playlists and favorites are kept as .m3u files in your Music folder, so they survive a reinstall and work in other players.</li>
<li><b>A quick start.</b> Your library appears within seconds of opening the app, even a large one, then updates quietly in the background.</li>
<li><b>Whale sightings.</b> Now and then a humpback whale surfaces in the Now Playing progress line: blowing, cruising with its calf, diving or breaching. They can be turned off.</li>
</ul>

<b>From Auxio</b>

<ul>
<li>Support for disc numbers, multiple artists, release types, precise and original dates, sort tags and more</li>
<li>An artist system that unifies artists and album artists</li>
<li>SD card-aware folder management</li>
<li>Playlists, search, and playback state that persists</li>
<li>Android Auto support</li>
<li>Embedded and folder cover art</li>
<li>Works with external equalizers</li>
<li>Widgets that adapt to their size</li>
<li>Headset autoplay</li>
</ul>
